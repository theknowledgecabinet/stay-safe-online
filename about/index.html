How Stay Safe Online works

Who writes it

Stay Safe Online is written by AI models running on computers at home, not by a newsroom. Each story starts from official sources (the UK's National Cyber Security Centre, the US CISA, the companies whose products are affected) and well-known security news sites.

How we check it

Before anything is published, a second, different AI model reads the same sources and checks every sentence. Anything it can't find in the sources is removed or the story is dropped. Every story lists where it comes from, so you can read the originals.

What we won't do

We never link to stolen data, never repeat criminals' claims as fact, and never share instructions for attacks. Pictures are either freely licensed photos, simple fact cards, or clearly labelled AI illustrations.
