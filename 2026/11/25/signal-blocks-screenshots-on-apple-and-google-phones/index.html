Signal blocks screenshots on Apple and Google phones

Signal is stopping screenshots by default on iPhones and Android phones to protect your messages from AI tools.

Wednesday 25 November 2026, 6.18am

What happened

The messaging app Signal has announced it will stop users from taking screenshots of messages by default. This change applies to people using Apple and Google operating systems. It follows similar restrictions Signal put in place for Microsoft users last year.

Signal says this move is needed because of concerns about how tech giants use artificial intelligence. AI agents are tools that can scan what is on your screen to answer questions or perform tasks. Signal’s president, Meredith Whittaker, said these agents are making the environment "increasingly hostile to privacy".

Whittaker warned that these AI tools capture data and send it to servers outside of Signal’s control. This could put the privacy of everyone in a conversation at risk. She described the situation as an "existential threat" to the app's mission.

You can still take screenshots if you want to. You can turn off the block in the app’s settings. However, Signal warns that this fix is not perfect. It does not stop AI agents from accessing data in other ways, such as reading files already saved on your device.

Am I affected?

This affects people who use the Signal app on iPhones or Android phones. If you use Signal, you will notice that you cannot take screenshots of messages by default. You can check your settings to see if the block is active.

What to do

  • Check your Signal settings to see if the screenshot block is on.
  • If you need to take a screenshot, you can turn off the block in the settings.
  • Be careful about what you share in chats, as AI tools may still access data in other ways.
