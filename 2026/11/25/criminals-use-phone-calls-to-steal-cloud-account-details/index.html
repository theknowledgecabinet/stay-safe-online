Criminals use phone calls to steal cloud account details

A security firm says criminals are calling businesses to trick staff into giving up access to their online accounts.

Wednesday 25 November 2026, 4.02pm

What happened

Palo Alto Networks, a security company, has reported a rise in phone-based scams. It says these attacks have been happening since August 2026. The criminals call people at work and use social engineering to trick them. Social engineering is a way of manipulating people into doing something they shouldn't.

The company says these calls have led to stolen data and attempts to demand money. The targets included businesses in the financial, healthcare, and legal sectors. The attacks affected companies in the US, the UK, and Australia. Other industries targeted included construction.

The security firm says it is tracking these 'waves' of attacks. It has also responded to them. The goal of the criminals is to hijack cloud accounts. A cloud account is an online login that lets you access files and services stored on the internet, rather than on a physical computer.

Am I affected?

The sources say the attacks targeted financial services, healthcare, legal services, and construction in the US, UK, and Australia. If you work in these sectors, check if your organisation has received any unusual phone calls or security alerts. You can also check your own account emails for signs of unauthorised access.

What to do

  • Be careful of unexpected phone calls asking for account details or passwords.
  • Verify the identity of the caller before sharing any information.
  • Report any suspicious calls to your organisation's IT or security team immediately.
