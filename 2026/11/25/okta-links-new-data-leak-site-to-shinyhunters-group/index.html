Okta links new data leak site to ShinyHunters group

Security firm Okta says a group linked to ShinyHunters may have rebranded as INCEPTION and is using voice calls to steal login details.

Wednesday 25 November 2026, 4.03pm

What happened

Okta’s Threat Intelligence team has identified a new threat cluster called O-UNC-053. The firm says this group is likely connected to the ShinyHunters extortion crew. It appears the group has rebranded itself under the name INCEPTION.

The group uses 'vishing' attacks. This is a type of voice phishing where criminals call you to trick you into giving away your login details. They may also ask you to approve requests on fake web pages.

Okta has linked this activity to a new data leak site called INCEPTION. A data leak site is a website where stolen information is often published or sold. The sources do not provide specific details on which companies or individuals have been targeted by this specific cluster.

Am I affected?

The sources don't say who is affected. You can check your account emails for unusual activity, review your bank statements for unauthorised payments, and look for official notices from services you use.

What to do

  • Be wary of unexpected phone calls asking for your login details or approval for requests.
  • Never enter your password on a page you did not navigate to yourself.
  • Check your email regularly for security alerts from your service providers.
