Ofcom checks X's handling of illegal content and Grok changes

Ofcom is monitoring X's response to hate and terror content and scrutinising data from its first quarterly report, while continuing its investigation into the Grok AI chatbot.

Wednesday 25 November 2026, 6.19am

What happened

Ofcom, the UK's online safety regulator, has received X's first quarterly report on how it handles reports of illegal hate and terror content. The regulator says the data shows some improvements in how quickly X reviews these reports. However, Ofcom still has concerns about how effectively X removes this illegal material from its platform.

Ofcom is also continuing its investigation into X over the Grok AI chatbot. The chatbot was used to create and share demeaning sexual deepfakes of real people, including children, which may amount to illegal content. As a result of Ofcom's pressure, X has made changes to ensure that content labelled as pornography is consistently restricted for accounts that have not completed age assurance.

Separately, SpaceXAI, the company behind the standalone Grok service, has introduced new restrictions. It now blocks people with UK IP addresses from viewing, generating and/or sharing pornographic content using the image generation technology provided by standalone Grok. It has also implemented restrictions to its companion chatbots to prevent them from engaging in sexually explicit conversations with UK users.

Ofcom will continue to monitor these changes. It plans to publish a further analysis of X's performance in the new year once it has received more data from the company.

Am I affected?

The sources state that Ofcom is monitoring the application of restrictions on the standalone Grok service and X's age checks. If you use these services in the UK, you may notice that certain content is now restricted or that age checks are required to view specific material.

What to do

  • Check your X account settings to ensure your age verification status is up to date if you wish to view adult content.
  • Be aware that the standalone Grok service now blocks UK users from generating or viewing pornographic images.
  • Report any illegal content you see on X using the platform's dedicated reporting tools.
