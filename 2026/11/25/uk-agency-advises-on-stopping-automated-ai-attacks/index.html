UK agency advises on stopping automated AI attacks

The NCSC has published new guidance to help organisations defend against automated attacks from AI tools.

Wednesday 25 November 2026, 4.01pm

What happened

The National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) has shared advice to tackle a rise in automated attacks. These attacks use AI frameworks, such as ARTEX, to probe for weaknesses in digital systems.

The NCSC says these tools do not usually find new security flaws. Instead, they exploit common mistakes, such as weak passwords or settings that are left open by default. The agency notes that these attacks can be fast and intense.

The guidance suggests eight steps for security teams. These include checking what services are visible to the public and locking down access. It also recommends using strong login checks and monitoring for unusual activity to spot these automated tools early.

The NCSC advises that while AI defence is the future, basic security hygiene is the priority now. Organisations should follow established standards, such as Cyber Essentials, to build a strong foundation.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. The guidance is aimed at organisations and security teams managing digital infrastructure. If you work in IT, you should check your organisation's security policies. If you are a home user, you can check your account emails and bank statements for signs of unusual activity.

What to do

  • Use strong, unique passwords for all your accounts.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication where available.
  • Keep your devices and apps updated with the latest security fixes.
