Samsung Securities data breach exposes customer details

Personal details for 8,241 customers have been exposed online.

Monday 23 November 2026, 8.56pm

What happened

South Korean firm Samsung Securities has suffered a data breach. The incident affected 8,241 customers. Personal information, including names, home addresses, and account numbers, was exposed online.

Times Now News reports that cyberattacks are rising globally. It notes that companies are finding it difficult to keep customer data safe thanks to AI.

Am I affected?

The sources say the breach affected 8,241 customers of Samsung Securities. You are only affected if you hold an account with this specific South Korean firm. If you do not use Samsung Securities, you are not affected by this specific incident.

What to do

  • Check your bank statements for any unrecognised transactions.
  • Be wary of calls or emails asking for your account details, as scammers may use the leaked data.
  • Keep your passwords unique and use two-factor authentication where available.
