Email security firm N-able hit by cyberattack

A cyberattack caused a multi-day outage for N-able's email filtering services, though the company says customer data was not compromised.

Monday 23 November 2026, 8.38pm

What happened

N-able, a provider of email security services, experienced a major disruption to its Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud services. The issue began on 16 November 2026 and affected customers across the Americas, Asia-Pacific, and Europe.

Initially, the company blamed the problem on technical configuration issues. However, on 18 November, N-able confirmed that a cyberattack was the cause. The company said it found no evidence that customer data or mailboxes were accessed by the attackers.

During the outage, email delivery and spam filtering continued to work. However, users could not access the management dashboard, view quarantined messages, or make changes to their settings. N-able has since taken steps to secure its systems and is working to restore full access to its tools.

The company stated that this incident is unrelated to a separate security issue affecting its N-central platform. It has provided specific guidance to customers based on how they use its services and continues to investigate the breach.

Am I affected?

The sources say the disruption affected Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud services across the Americas, Asia-Pacific, and Europe. If you use these specific email security tools, check your email for notices from N-able. You can also check if you can log in to your management dashboard to see if access has been restored.

What to do

  • Check your email for any official updates or guidance from N-able.
  • If you use Mail Assure or SpamExperts, try to log in to your management portal to see if it is working.
  • Keep your passwords up to date and use strong, unique passwords for your email accounts.
