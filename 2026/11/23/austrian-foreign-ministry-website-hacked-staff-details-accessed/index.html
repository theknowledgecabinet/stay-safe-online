Austrian Foreign Ministry website hacked, staff details accessed

Hackers accessed the names and contact details of diplomats at the Austrian Foreign Ministry, though no public data was stolen.

Monday 23 November 2026, 9.00pm

What happened

Austria's Foreign Ministry said hackers breached one of its websites between 12 and 13 November. The breach affected a site run by an external service provider. The ministry confirmed that the names and official contact information of employees in Vienna and abroad were accessed.

The ministry said the breach has been contained. It stated that no personal information belonging to members of the public was compromised. It is not yet clear how the hackers gained access or if they stole any other information.

The incident happened around the same time as a global Microsoft service outage. Microsoft said that outage was caused by a software bug. Austrian authorities have not established whether the two events are connected.

A ministry spokesperson said the incident is being taken "very seriously". They noted that the attack has not been attributed to any specific group. The ministry has notified Austria's cybersecurity and data protection authorities.

Am I affected?

The sources say the breach affected the names and official contact details of employees at the Austrian Foreign Ministry. The ministry stated that no personal information belonging to members of the public was compromised. If you are not an employee of that ministry, you are not affected by this specific breach.

What to do

  • Check your email for any unexpected messages claiming to be from government officials.
  • Do not click links or download attachments in suspicious emails.
  • Keep your devices and apps up to date with the latest security fixes.
