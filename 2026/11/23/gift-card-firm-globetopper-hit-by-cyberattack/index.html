Gift card firm GlobeTopper hit by cyberattack

A business that sells digital gift cards to other companies suffered a cyberattack, leading to lost revenue and redeemed cards.

Monday 23 November 2026, 8.43pm

What happened

GlobeTopper is a company that helps other businesses sell digital gift cards. It works with over 4,000 brands in 65 countries, including IKEA, Adidas, and Spotify. A cyberattack disrupted its operations in late October 2026.

The parent company, iQSTEL, said the attack was detected on 22 October. The company quickly shut down its platform to investigate. It took until 31 October to rebuild the system and restore services in stages from 1 November.

The attackers redeemed digital gift cards worth $70,000. GlobeTopper says it cannot recover these cards and will have to absorb the loss. The attack also caused an estimated $2.5 million in lost revenue for the company.

The company says the attackers got hold of certain access keys used by its business customers. These keys have been cancelled and replaced. An investigation is ongoing to check if any other personal or payment data was accessed.

Am I affected?

The sources say the attackers obtained access keys for certain business customers of GlobeTopper. If you use a service that relies on GlobeTopper for gift cards, you should check for any unusual activity on your account. The sources do not say if personal customer data was stolen.

What to do

  • Check your email for any unexpected messages about your accounts.
  • Review your bank statements for any unrecognised charges.
  • Look for official notices from the services you use to see if they have been affected.
