US retailer The Container Store hit by cyberattack

A US home goods retailer has restored its online systems after a cyberattack forced it to pause digital ordering.

Monday 23 November 2026, 9.02pm

What happened

The Container Store, a US-based retailer with about 100 stores, said it is recovering from a cyberattack. The incident forced the company to shut down its online ordering systems and limit in-store transaction processing. Its parent company, Neighborhood Intelligence, confirmed that the website and systems have now been restored.

During the disruption, stores remained open but had to use alternative methods to process transactions. The company said it hired cybersecurity experts to contain the incident and restore the affected systems. It added that the incident only impacted The Container Store and did not affect its other brands, such as Bed Bath & Beyond.

A cybercriminal group called n0n listed the company on its leak site earlier this month. The company has not yet addressed these claims. So far, it does not appear that customer financial data was stolen, but the firm has not confirmed this definitively.

Cybersecurity experts say the n0n group specialises in ransomware and data extortion. They have attacked more than 100 organisations and demanded large sums of money. The group has primarily targeted healthcare and pharmaceuticals but recently claimed to have data from retail firms.

Am I affected?

The sources say the incident only impacted The Container Store and did not affect its other brands. It does not appear that customer financial data was stolen, but the company has not confirmed this. If you shopped at The Container Store, check your bank statements and account emails for any unusual activity.

What to do

  • Check your bank statements and credit card bills for any unrecognised charges.
  • Look for emails from The Container Store or its parent company for official updates.
  • Be wary of phishing - fake messages that try to trick you into giving away details - especially if they claim to be from the retailer.
