Teenager held for trying to sell private data of 4,000 Agniveers

A 14-year-old boy in India has been detained after allegedly stealing personal details from a government website.

Monday 23 November 2026, 6.06am

What happened

Investigators in India say a 14-year-old boy gained unauthorised access to a test website. This site was designed for former Agniveers, a specific group of soldiers, to register for job opportunities in other government departments.

According to the reports, the boy allegedly extracted personal information from the site. He then posted an advertisement on the dark web, claiming he had the private data of 4,000 Agniveers.

The dark web is a hidden part of the internet where illegal activities often take place. The boy is now being held by investigators for this alleged offence.

Am I affected?

The sources say this incident affects former Agniveers in India. The sources don't say who is affected in the UK. You can check your own account emails or bank statements for any unusual activity to be safe.

What to do

  • Keep your passwords unique for different websites.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication where available.
  • Check your email regularly for unexpected messages.
