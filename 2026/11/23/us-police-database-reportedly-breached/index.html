US police database reportedly breached

US police databases may have been hacked.

Monday 23 November 2026, 8.29pm

What happened

US news agencies report that the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) is investigating a security breach. The incident reportedly targeted the National Crime Information Center (NCIC). This is a database used by US police to share details about wanted people and missing items.

The FBI has not confirmed the breach or said what information was accessed. It is also unclear how the attackers got in or if any data was stolen. The incident happened just two months after a previous attack on the FBI's recruitment website.

There is no evidence that this breach is connected to the earlier attack. The FBI is currently looking into the matter, but officials have not released further details. The US government has faced several cyber incidents this year, but this specific event involves US law enforcement systems.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. The breach reportedly targeted a US law enforcement database. You should check your own account emails and bank statements for any unusual activity, as is good practice.

What to do

  • Keep your phone and computer software up to date.
  • Be careful of emails asking for personal details, as these may be phishing - fake messages that try to trick you.
  • Check your bank statements regularly for any unrecognised transactions.
