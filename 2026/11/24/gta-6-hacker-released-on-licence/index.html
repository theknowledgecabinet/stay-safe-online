GTA 6 hacker released on licence

A man who hacked the maker of Grand Theft Auto has been released on licence, with a warning to not let it happen again.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 8.57pm

What happened

A cyber criminal who hacked the maker of Grand Theft Auto has been released on licence. The report notes that this sentence may not deter young people engaging in cybercrime. It suggests the outcome could be seen as a free pass by those considering similar offences.

Am I affected?

The sources don't say who is affected. Check your account emails, your bank statements, and the service's own notices for any signs of unauthorised access.

What to do

  • Keep your passwords unique and strong for each account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication where available.
  • Report any suspicious activity to the relevant service immediately.
