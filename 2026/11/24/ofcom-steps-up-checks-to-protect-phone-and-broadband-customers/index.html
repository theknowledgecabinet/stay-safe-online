Ofcom steps up checks to protect phone and broadband customers

The UK regulator is working harder to ensure providers treat customers fairly, from fixing price hikes to making complaints easier to handle.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 6.19am

What happened

Ofcom, the UK’s communications regulator, has updated how it oversees phone and broadband providers. It is now engaging with a wider range of companies, including newer entrants, to check they follow the rules.

In the last 18 months, Ofcom has secured several changes. It made sure O2 corrected information about price rises for customers still in contract. It also worked with 10 providers to make it easier for customers to raise complaints and understand what happens next.

The regulator has also made early termination charges clearer. This means customers cancelling within their minimum contract term are less likely to be overcharged. Ofcom also stopped two smaller providers from automatically adding extras, like anti-virus software, during online sales.

If a provider does not improve after initial talks, Ofcom can take formal action. In July 2026, it fined Virgin Media £28 million because customers struggled to cancel their broadband services.

Am I affected?

You can check if your provider has made these improvements by looking at their website for clear information on price rises and how to make a complaint. You can also check your bills to ensure you are not being charged for extras you did not ask for.

What to do

  • Check your provider’s website for clear information on how to make a complaint and any price changes.
  • Review your contract to understand your early termination charges before cancelling.
  • Look for Ofcom’s Consumer Hub for advice on saving money and handling service problems.
