US state shuts down myColorado app after data breach

The myColorado app is offline while officials investigate a breach that may have exposed data for up to 150,000 people.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 8.54pm

What happened

The myColorado app is currently offline. This follows a data breach where an unauthorised user accessed information on the platform. The app belongs to the US state of Colorado.

Officials found the problem on 18 November. The Governor’s Office of Information Technology confirmed that someone had gained unauthorised access to the data. The app will remain shut down while the investigation continues.

Up to 150,000 people may be affected by this breach. The exact number of people whose details were seen is not yet known. Officials are working to understand the full scale of the incident.

Am I affected?

The sources say up to 150,000 people may be affected, but do not list specific names or UK residents. You should check if you use the myColorado app or have an account with the Colorado state government. If you do not use this service, you are likely not affected.

What to do

  • Check if you have an account on the myColorado app.
  • Monitor your bank statements and credit reports for any unusual activity.
  • Be careful of phishing emails asking for your login details, as scammers may use this news to trick you.
