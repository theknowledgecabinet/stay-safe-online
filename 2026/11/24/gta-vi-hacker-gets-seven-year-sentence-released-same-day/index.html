GTA VI hacker gets seven-year sentence, released same day

A British man who hacked Rockstar Games and leaked game footage was sentenced to prison but released immediately due to time already served.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 8.57pm

What happened

Arion Kurtaj, 21, was sentenced to seven years in prison for hacking Rockstar Games and leaking footage of Grand Theft Auto VI. He was released the same day because he had already spent time in a psychiatric hospital.

Kurtaj admitted to six offences, including four under the Computer Misuse Act. He carried out the hacks in 2022 while on bail for earlier crimes. Police said he used an Amazon Fire Stick plugged into a hotel television to break into the systems of Uber, Revolut, and Rockstar Games.

The judge reduced his initial nine-year sentence by two years. Kurtaj will now serve the rest of his sentence under supervision in the community. He can be returned to prison if he breaks the conditions of his release.

Police are applying for an order to restrict his activities. A five-year order has already been imposed, banning him from using the messaging apps Telegram and Discord.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You can check your account emails for unusual activity, review your bank statements for unrecognised charges, and look for official notices from services you use.

What to do

  • Keep your passwords unique and use a password manager to store them safely.
  • Be careful of messages asking for personal details, as these may be phishing attempts.
  • Check that your devices are up to date with the latest security updates.
