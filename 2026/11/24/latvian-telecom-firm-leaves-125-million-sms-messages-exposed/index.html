Latvian telecom firm leaves 125 million SMS messages exposed

A database containing over 125 million text messages from a Latvian telecom provider was left open to the public.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 6.19am

What happened

CSC Telecom, a telecommunications provider in Latvia, left a database exposed. It contained more than 125 million SMS messages.

The records cover messages sent between October 2022 and September 2026.

The leak reportedly includes details for clients of several major companies. These include Swedbank, SEB, and airBaltic, along with dozens of other organisations.

Am I affected?

The sources say the leak includes clients of Swedbank, SEB, airBaltic, and dozens of other companies. They do not specify if UK customers are included. Check your account emails and bank statements for any unusual activity or notices from these services.

What to do

  • Check your bank and travel accounts for any unauthorised transactions or changes.
  • Watch for phishing emails or messages that ask for your personal details.
  • Change your passwords if you use the same one for multiple services.
  • Keep your phone and apps updated to protect against security threats.
