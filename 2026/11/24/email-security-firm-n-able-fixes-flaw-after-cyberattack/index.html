Email security firm N-able fixes flaw after cyberattack

N-able has patched a critical security flaw in its email services after a cyberattack disrupted access for some users.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 6.18am

What happened

N-able, a company that provides email security tools, confirmed that its Mail Assure and SpamExperts services were targeted in a cyberattack. The company said the attackers accessed a web server and likely reached certain credentials stored there. As a result, N-able temporarily disabled access to its management portal and other features to contain the incident.

During its investigation, the company found a critical vulnerability. This is a weakness in the software that allowed an authorised user to make the server connect to internal systems it should not have reached. N-able has now released a fix for this issue. The company states that the attack was limited to one specific server and that email delivery was not affected during the disruption.

Access to the services was restored on 21 November. N-able says it has found no evidence of further malicious activity since the fix was applied. The company is continuing its investigation and plans to share a detailed report on what happened within the next two weeks.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You can check if you use N-able’s Mail Assure or SpamExperts services by looking at your email provider or asking your IT team. If you do not use these specific business email tools, this incident likely does not affect you directly.

What to do

  • If you use N-able services, check your email for official notices from the company.
  • Keep your passwords unique for each account to protect yourself if credentials are ever compromised.
  • Enable two-factor authentication on your email accounts for an extra layer of security.
