Cyberattack disrupts hospital systems in Guadeloupe

A cyberattack has forced a hospital in the French overseas department of Guadeloupe to delay non-emergency procedures.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 6.18am

What happened

A major cyberattack hit the university hospital in Guadeloupe on Saturday night. The attack disrupted the hospital's information systems and telephone lines.

Staff were unable to access several applications and databases. This forced the hospital to activate its emergency response plan. As a result, non-emergency procedures have been postponed.

The incident was reported by RFI.

Am I affected?

Staff were affected, as they were unable to access several applications and databases. You can check your account emails, bank statements, and the service's own notices for any relevant updates.

What to do

  • Keep your phone and computer software up to date.
  • Be careful with unexpected calls or messages asking for personal details.
  • Check official hospital websites for the latest updates if you have appointments there.
