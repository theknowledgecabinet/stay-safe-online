Billions of passwords exposed in two database leaks

Two separate online databases left billions of usernames and passwords open to the public, according to new findings.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 8.42pm

What happened

Researchers found two unrelated databases. These databases held over 3.5 billion usernames, passwords, and national identity numbers.

The data was left exposed to anyone on the internet. The findings were shared with WIRED magazine.

The leaks included documents and personal information. It is not yet clear exactly which services these databases belonged to. The reports describe the scale of the exposure rather than the specific cause.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You can check your account emails for security alerts, review your bank statements for unusual activity, and look for notices from the services you use.

What to do

  • Change your passwords for any accounts you use on multiple websites.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication where possible to add an extra layer of security.
  • Watch for signs of phishing, which are fake messages that try to trick you into giving away details.
