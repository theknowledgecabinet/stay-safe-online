Hong Kong hospital pauses app after data breach

A hospital in Hong Kong has paused its patient app after finding that personal details were accessed without permission.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 8.27pm

What happened

Gleneagles Hospital in Hong Kong has stopped using its patient mobile app. The hospital said it found unauthorised activity on 18 November. This involved the security systems that support the app called My Gleneagles.

The hospital stated that personal data was accessed without permission. This included patients' names, identity document numbers, and contact details.

The hospital posted a statement on its website on Tuesday. It confirmed the detection of the unauthorised access. No further details were provided in the source material.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You can check your account emails for notices, review your bank statements for unusual activity, and look for official alerts from services you use.

What to do

  • Check your email for any official notices from the service.
  • Review your bank statements for any unrecognised transactions.
  • Keep your phone and apps updated to the latest version.
