UK cyber agency warns of rise in AI-assisted attacks

The National Cyber Security Centre says attackers are using AI tools to find and exploit old security weaknesses faster.

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 8.56pm

What happened

Britain’s National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) has reported a marked rise in cyberattacks assisted by artificial intelligence since September. The agency says these attacks are not using new tricks. Instead, AI tools are helping criminals find and use existing security gaps much faster.

The NCSC notes that these tools make attacks cheaper and more persistent. However, the agency stresses that the weaknesses being exploited are not new. A forgotten password or an unprotected connection that might have gone unnoticed for years can now be found in minutes.

The centre warns that AI does not make standard security practices obsolete. In fact, it says building strong foundations matters more than ever. The NCSC states that nearly everything it has seen can be prevented by following basic cyber security best practice.

Am I affected?

The sources say organisations across multiple sectors are reporting incidents. The NCSC notes that weaknesses which might once have gone unnoticed can now be found quickly. To check yourself, review your account emails for unusual activity, check your bank statements, and look for notices from the services you use.

What to do

  • Keep your passwords strong and unique for each account.
  • Update your software and devices regularly to fix known security gaps.
  • Be careful with links and messages that ask for your details, as AI can make these scams more convincing.
  • Review your online security settings to ensure you have basic protections in place.
