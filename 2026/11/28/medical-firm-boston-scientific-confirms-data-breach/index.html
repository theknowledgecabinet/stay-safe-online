Medical firm Boston Scientific confirms data breach

A cyberattack exposed the personal and medical details of 117,789 people, prompting the company to send out warning letters.

Saturday 28 November 2026, 6.19am

What happened

Medical technology company Boston Scientific is sending notices to people affected by a recent cyberattack. According to a sample notification shared with US authorities, the attacker used a voice phishing call to trick staff into giving them access to the company's cloud systems. Voice phishing is a type of scam where a fraudster calls you to steal your information.

The attack exposed data between 7 May and 1 November. The company found out about the unauthorised access on 12 May and confirmed a data breach occurred on 5 November. The exposed information included full names, addresses, dates of birth, and medical details. It also included sensitive numbers like Social Security Numbers and government ID numbers.

Boston Scientific said the incident did not affect the safety or performance of its medical devices. It also said there was no disruption to manufacturing or patient services. The company has contained the incident and notified law enforcement. This is the second security incident for the firm in 2026, following a separate cyberattack in August that caused network outages.

The company is not offering free credit monitoring to those affected. Instead, it recommends that people use free credit reports and consider placing fraud alerts or credit freezes on their accounts to protect themselves.

Am I affected?

The sources say individuals in multiple US states are affected, with 117,789 people listed. The sources do not say if UK residents are affected.

What to do

  • Consider placing a fraud alert or credit freeze on your credit report as recommended by the company.
