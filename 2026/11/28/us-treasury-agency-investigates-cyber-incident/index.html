US Treasury agency investigates cyber incident

A US financial crime agency is investigating a cyber incident that led to unauthorised access to some of its systems.

Saturday 28 November 2026, 3.49pm

What happened

The US Treasury Department’s Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) said on 25 November it is investigating a cybersecurity incident. The agency stated that this resulted in unauthorised access to a limited subset of its systems.

FinCEN handles reports about financial crimes, including money laundering and terrorism financing. As a proactive measure, it has taken some services offline. However, the agency noted that its secure online filing system is still operational.

The agency has also extended deadlines for financial institutions to file required documents. FinCEN added that if a financial institution needs to send urgent information to law enforcement, they can use a specific hotline that is not affected by the disruption.

This incident comes as several other US federal agencies, including the FBI and the CIA, have recently started investigating data breaches. These breaches reportedly target criminal records and unclassified intelligence information.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You can check your account emails, bank statements, and the service's own notices for any updates.

What to do

  • Keep your passwords unique and use a password manager to store them safely.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication for your important accounts.
  • Be careful with unexpected emails or calls asking for personal details.
