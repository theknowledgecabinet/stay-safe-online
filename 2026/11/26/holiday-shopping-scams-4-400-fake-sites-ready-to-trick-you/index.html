Holiday shopping scams: 4,400 fake sites ready to trick you

Fortinet says criminals are using fake delivery sites and stolen data to target shoppers during the holiday season.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 3.57pm

What happened

Fortinet’s researchers found more than 4,400 malicious websites set up to trick holiday shoppers. These sites often look like delivery services or famous retailers. They may ask for payment for missed deliveries or customs fees.

Criminals are also using stolen data. Researchers found 1.72 million records from a Russian marketplace. These records contain login details and payment information for major online shops. Attackers can use this data to access accounts or make fake purchases.

Some fake websites do not look like the real brand at all. A separate study found that one in four fake pages used normal web platforms. This makes it harder for shoppers to spot the scam just by looking at the web address.

Criminals are also using AI tools. They use these to write convincing messages and create fake videos. This can make scams look more real and target specific groups of people.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You can check your bank statements for unrecognised charges. Look for emails from your online shops about account changes. Check the official website of any retailer you use to see if they have issued warnings.

What to do

  • Type the retailer's web address directly into your browser instead of clicking links in emails.
  • Check the web address carefully for spelling mistakes or unusual characters.
  • Do not enter payment details on sites that ask for fees for missed deliveries.
  • Keep your device software up to date to help block malicious software.
