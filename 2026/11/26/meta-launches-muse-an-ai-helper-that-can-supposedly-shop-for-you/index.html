Meta launches Muse, an AI helper that can supposedly shop for you

Meta has released a new AI tool that can manage tasks and make purchases, but UK consumers should be extremely cautious about relying on it.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 6.11am

What happened

Meta has launched Muse, a 'personal AI agent' designed to help users aged 18 and over with daily tasks. It works like a chatbot, allowing you to ask it to organise your calendar, plan holidays, or compare deals. It can also make purchases on your behalf, though you must approve each transaction before it happens.

Currently, Muse works with select UK retailers such as M&S, ASOS, and Currys. However, major sites like Amazon have blocked it. Which? found that using Muse for a test purchase cost around £15 more than buying directly from Amazon, as it missed free delivery and better prices. As with all AI chatbots, Muse can and will make mistakes, so relying on it too much could prove costly.

Privacy is a key concern. Muse needs access to personal data, such as emails and messages, to work effectively. Meta says it uses a secure system called MuseSecure VM to keep your data on your device. However, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) has urged caution over consumers sharing or giving access to too much personal data with Muse, including ‘sensitive information about yourself and information about other people’.

The Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) has urged consumers tempted to use Muse to consider carefully not only the information presented to them about the potential benefits of an AI agent, but also the information presented to them about risks and limitations. It is important to understand the risks and limitations before letting an AI agent act on your behalf.

Am I affected?

This tool is available to users aged 18 and over who choose to set it up. You can check if you have connected any services to Muse by going to its settings. The sources do not say who is affected if you do not use the tool.

What to do

  • Only connect services you trust and review the permissions you give Muse regularly.
  • Always double-check any purchase or action before you approve it, as the AI can make mistakes.
  • Use the settings to control what data Muse can access and to delete any chat history you no longer want.
  • Read the terms and conditions carefully to understand exactly what the AI is allowed to do.
