Most Black Friday deals are not the cheapest of the year

New research shows that most Black Friday discounts are not the best prices of the year, so you do not need to rush to buy.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 6.12am

What happened

A consumer group called Which? has published its largest-ever investigation into Black Friday sales. They tracked over a quarter of a million prices for 756 home, tech and health appliances across eight major UK retailers.

The research found that only 22% of products were at their lowest price of the year during the Black Friday sales period. For 44% of products, the sale price was the same as the lowest price seen at other times of the year.

Which? also found that 27% of products became cheaper in the six months after the sale. This means that for many items, you could have waited and paid less. The group says most deals do not need to be bought urgently because similar prices are likely to appear later.

The investigation covered retailers including Amazon, Argos, Currys and John Lewis. It compared prices during the Black Friday period with prices from the six months before and after the sale.

Am I affected?

This affects anyone planning to buy home, tech or health appliances during the Black Friday sales period.

What to do

  • Do not feel pressured to buy immediately, as similar prices often return after the sale.
  • Look for retailer price guarantees, but check the specific terms and conditions.
  • Wait until after the sale if you are not in a rush, as some products may drop in price further.
