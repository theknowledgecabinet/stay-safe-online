Ofcom warns of five common Christmas phone and text scams

Ofcom has listed five festive scams to watch for, urging people to check details before clicking links or sending money.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 3.56pm

What happened

Ofcom is warning the public to stay alert as fraudsters use the busy Christmas period to steal money and personal information. The regulator says criminals often create a sense of urgency to trick people into making quick decisions. This is a common tactic during the festive season when many people are expecting parcels or looking for bargains.

The five scams to watch out for include fake texts about missed parcel deliveries, bogus Christmas giveaways, and messages claiming there is a suspicious purchase on your account. Scammers may also pretend to be a family member who has broken their phone and needs urgent cash. Finally, fake seasonal job offers may ask for upfront fees or personal documents.

Ofcom advises that you should never click links in suspicious messages. Instead, open the official app or website yourself to check your account or delivery status. If you receive a message from an unknown number claiming to be a loved one, contact them through another method to verify their identity.

Amy Jordan, Ofcom’s Strategy Delivery Director, said: “Taking a few seconds to stop and think before you click could save you a lot of worry and stress.” Ofcom has also launched a new online Consumer Hub with tips on how to protect yourself against scams.

Am I affected?

Ofcom says four in ten mobile phone users have received a suspicious message and a third have received a suspicious call. If you have lost money or been hacked, report it to the police.

What to do

  • Never click on links in suspicious texts or emails.
  • Check your account or delivery status by opening the official app or website yourself.
  • Contact family members through another method if you receive an urgent money request from an unknown number.
  • Report any fraud to the police or the relevant fraud reporting service.
