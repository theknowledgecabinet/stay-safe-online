Ransomware attacks jump 23% in October, report finds

A new report shows a sharp rise in ransomware attacks, driven by the use of AI to find weaknesses faster.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 6.10am

What happened

Ransomware attacks increased by 23% in October compared to September. A report by NCC Group found 1,049 claimed incidents during the month. This follows the highest ever quarterly volume of attacks in the previous three months.

The researchers linked this rise to the growing use of AI. They said AI helps attackers rapidly identify vulnerabilities and plan attacks. NCC Group wrote that this spike is likely an early indicator of a new cyber environment where serious flaws are found and used more quickly.

The Gentlemen ransomware group claimed the highest number of victims in October. A newer group called N0n was close behind, claiming 133 victims. NCC Group noted that the origins of these newer groups remain unknown. They said these should be described as newly observed brands rather than entirely new threat actors.

Industrials was the sector most heavily targeted, making up 31% of claimed attacks. North America was the most targeted region, accounting for 46% of attacks. Europe followed at 23%.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You can check your account emails, your bank statements, and the service's own notices for any signs of a breach.

What to do

  • Keep your software up to date to fix known weaknesses.
  • Be careful with messages that ask for personal details, as attackers use AI to find ways in.
  • Check your online accounts regularly for any unusual activity.
