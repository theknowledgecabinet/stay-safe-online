Todoist confirms data breach affecting some users

The task management app says an attacker accessed its systems and downloaded details for a small number of accounts.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 3.58pm

What happened

Todoist said an attacker breached its production systems on 23 November 2026. The company stated that the attacker downloaded account records for about 6% of its users. These records included email addresses, account creation dates, and older password hashes. A hash is a scrambled version of a password used to check it without storing the actual word.

The attacker also obtained integration tokens and accessed some data from connected Outlook mailboxes. Todoist said it blocked the attacker’s access and began notifying affected users on 24 November. The company found no sign that the attacker logged in to accounts in the group affected by the old password hashes.

Todoist traced the breach to a stolen credential linked to its code management system. The attacker used this to change automated workflows and take over an employee’s work account. Security monitoring flagged an unexpected administrator account hours after the data was downloaded. The company has since removed the attacker’s accounts and replaced exposed credentials.

Todoist says users who did not receive a notice do not need to act. If the company disabled your password, you should use the official reset page. The company also advises checking if you stored any other keys or credentials in your tasks, as deleting them will not remove copies the attacker may have taken.

Am I affected?

Todoist says users who did not receive a notice do not need to act. If you received a notice, check your email for messages from no-reply@notify.todoist.com. If your password was disabled, you will need to reset it.

What to do

  • Check your email for a notice from no-reply@notify.todoist.com.
  • If your password was disabled, use the official Todoist password reset page.
  • Change that password anywhere else you have reused it.
  • If you stored API keys or other credentials in Todoist, revoke and replace them with the service that issued them.
