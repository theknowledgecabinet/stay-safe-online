FIFA confirms hackers stole limited personal data

The football governing body says its systems were breached, affecting a small amount of personal and business information.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 6.09am

What happened

FIFA has confirmed that hackers accessed its internal systems. The organisation says they gained unauthorised access to a limited set of personal and business information.

A spokesperson said FIFA is working with law enforcement. They condemned the theft of personal data as a serious violation of privacy.

A cybercriminal group called the Handsome Boy Hacking Club claims it stole the data. The group says it tried to extort FIFA but failed, so it published the information online.

FIFA did not say exactly what data was taken or who is affected. It did not respond to questions about the nature of the incident.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You should check if you have any connection to FIFA, such as being an employee or a specific business partner. If you are unsure, look for official notices from the organisation or check your bank statements for any unusual activity.

What to do

  • Watch for official communications from FIFA if you have a direct link to the organisation.
  • Keep your passwords unique and use a password manager to protect your accounts.
  • Be careful of phishing emails claiming to be from FIFA asking for your details.
