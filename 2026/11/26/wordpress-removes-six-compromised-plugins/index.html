WordPress removes six compromised plugins

WordPress removed six compromised plugins before they could be released to the public.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 4.00pm

What happened

WordPress has removed six plugins made by a developer called WP Connect. This happened on 22 November 2026.

The plugins contained hidden code, known as a backdoor. This code could have allowed criminals to run harmful commands on websites that used the plugins.

The WordPress team found the problem on its own. They blocked the updates before the malicious versions were officially released to users.

The removal followed a suspected compromise of the developer's account. This means the developer's login details may have been stolen.

Am I affected?

The sources say the malicious versions were blocked before they could be officially released. Therefore, you likely do not have the compromised version installed. You can check your website's plugin list to ensure you are using the latest safe versions.

What to do

  • Check your website's plugin list to ensure you do not have the six WP Connect plugins installed.
  • Keep all your website plugins up to date with the latest official versions.
  • Review your website's security settings to ensure no unknown changes have been made.
