AI used in new bank fraud attacks in India

Indian authorities warn that criminals are using AI to trick banks and tech firms into making wrong payments.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 4.01pm

What happened

The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) has issued a warning about new cyber attacks. These attacks use artificial intelligence (AI) to target banks and technology companies in India.

According to CERT-In, this activity has led to unauthorised financial transactions. This means money was moved without proper permission from banking and financial services entities.

The warning highlights that threat actors are carrying out AI-assisted cyber attacks targeting information and communication technology (ICT) assets in the country. This unsanctioned activity has resulted in unauthorized financial transactions at banking and financial services entities, as well as technology service providers.

While this specific warning targets Indian institutions, it serves as a reminder that fraud is becoming more sophisticated globally.

Am I affected?

The sources say the attacks target banking and financial services entities in India. They do not say if UK readers are affected. You should check your bank statements and account emails for any unusual activity.

What to do

  • Review your bank statements regularly for any transactions you do not recognise.
  • Be careful of messages asking for payment details, as scammers may use AI to sound convincing.
  • Contact your bank immediately if you see any unauthorised payments.
