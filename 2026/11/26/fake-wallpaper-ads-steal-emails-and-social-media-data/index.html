Fake wallpaper ads steal emails and social media data

Fake wallpaper ads are installing software that steals emails and social media details from your computer.

Thursday 26 November 2026, 3.54pm

What happened

Security firm Bitdefender has found a new type of malware called SYS010. It arrives through fake ads on Facebook and other Meta platforms. These ads promise free animated wallpapers for your Windows computer.

The wallpaper works as promised, which hides the fact that the software is doing something else in the background. It forces your web browsers to install a fake add-on. This add-on looks like a document tool but is actually designed to steal your data.

The malware can read your recent Gmail and Outlook emails. It can also permanently delete messages. This means it could hide security alerts or steal login codes before you see them. It also steals details from your Facebook business accounts, including payment information.

Bitdefender says the campaign is still active. It has reached thousands of people in Europe and other regions. The researchers warn that this is a serious upgrade to older malware they have seen since 2022.

Am I affected?

The sources say the ads target people in Europe, Asia, North America, Africa, and South America. You may be affected if you downloaded a free wallpaper from a social media ad. Check your browser for a message saying it is 'managed by your organisation' if you do not work for a company that manages your devices.

What to do

  • Do not download software from social media ads.
  • Refuse any pop-up asking for administrator permission if you do not know what it is for.
  • Check your browser for a 'managed by your organisation' message and investigate if you see one on a personal computer.
  • Review who has access to your social media accounts and remove permissions you no longer need.
