Gingko Bioworks data breach claim by cybercriminal group

A cybercriminal group claims to have stolen data from US biotech firm Gingko Bioworks, but the company has not confirmed the details.

Friday 27 November 2026, 6.21am

What happened

Gingko Bioworks Holdings issued a statement on 8 October about a data breach. However, getting an update from the company has been difficult since then.

On or about 19 November, a group called FulcrumSec added Gingko Bioworks to its leak site. This is a website where cybercriminals publish stolen information to prove their claims.

The group says it acquired 9.9 terabytes of data. This is a very large amount of digital information. They claim to have taken 10,775,976 files from 82 different storage areas, known as buckets.

The group provided detailed analysis of the data they say they acquired. However, these are only the claims of the cybercriminals. The company has not confirmed the specific amounts or the nature of the stolen files.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You can check if you are affected by looking for emails from the company, checking your bank statements for unusual activity, or looking for official notices on the company's website.

What to do

  • Check your email for any official communications from Gingko Bioworks.
  • Monitor your bank statements for any unrecognised transactions.
  • Be wary of phishing emails that claim to be from the company asking for your details.
