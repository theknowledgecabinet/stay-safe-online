Barclays US arm confirms credit card data breach

Barclays Bank Delaware is telling customers that their credit card application details were exposed in a data breach.

Friday 27 November 2026, 3.55pm

What happened

Barclays Bank Delaware, the US banking arm of the British bank Barclays, has confirmed a data breach. The incident involved credit card application data for American consumers.

The bank has started informing affected customers directly. A breach notice was also shared with authorities, according to reports.

The sources do not provide further details on how the breach happened or exactly which specific data points were taken beyond the application information.

Am I affected?

The sources say the breach affects customers of Barclays Bank Delaware in the US. You can tell if you are affected by checking if you have received a direct notice from the bank. If you do not have a US Barclays account, the sources do not say you are affected.

What to do

  • Check your email and post for an official notice from Barclays Bank Delaware.
  • Monitor your credit card statements for any unrecognised transactions.
  • Be wary of phishing - fake messages that try to trick you into giving away details - if you hear about this breach.
