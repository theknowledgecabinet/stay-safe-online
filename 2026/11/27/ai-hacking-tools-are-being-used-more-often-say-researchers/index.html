AI hacking tools are being used more often, say researchers

New data shows a sharp rise in the use of AI tools to launch cyber attacks, but experts say traditional security postures will not keep pace.

Friday 27 November 2026, 3.59pm

What happened

A company called Infrawatch says the use of an open-source AI hacking tool called Artex has surged in November. It tracked more than 130 different versions of the tool being used in the wild. This is a big jump from early in the month, when it saw about 3,200 servers running these tools. By late November, that number had more than doubled.

The researchers say these attacks are often run automatically by AI agents. They use popular AI models like Claude and DeepSeek to find weaknesses in computer systems. However, the attacks are not always successful. Infrawatch says the success rate is roughly a coin flip, partly because the AI tools sometimes make mistakes or 'hallucinate' information that is not true.

Importantly, the researchers found that these attacks do not use new, secret tricks. They rely on existing weaknesses, such as software that has not been updated or settings that are not secure. The researchers state that traditional security posture will not keep pace with these attacks. However, the infrastructure used to launch these attacks is visible and can be blocked by security teams identifying and blocking that infrastructure.

There are also reports linking this tool to incidents at US government agencies, specifically the CIA, the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, and the National Geospatial Intelligence Agency, though these are not officially confirmed. Experts warn that these tools are becoming easier for criminals to use. They may soon be offered as a service, making it easier for people without technical skills to launch attacks.

Am I affected?

The sources name the CIA, the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, and the National Geospatial Intelligence Agency as affected agencies.

What to do

  • Identify and block the infrastructure used to launch attacks.
  • Patch vulnerabilities and fix misconfigurations.
