Switzerland confirms first cyberattacks using AI agents

Swiss authorities have confirmed that AI tools were used to successfully hack local organisations, warning that these attacks are faster and harder to stop.

Friday 27 November 2026, 6.15am

What happened

Switzerland’s National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) has confirmed its first reports of cyberattacks carried out with the help of AI agents. These attacks targeted organisations and critical infrastructure operators in at least two sectors. In every case known to the agency, the AI-assisted attacks successfully compromised the victims' IT systems.

The attackers used software known as 'agentic offensive frameworks'. Unlike a chatbot that only gives advice, these AI agents can search for weaknesses, choose attack tools, and decide what to do next with very little human input. The NCSC says these tools can find and exploit standard weaknesses, such as easy-to-guess passwords, much faster than a human could.

The agency warns that the number of these AI agents available on the internet has nearly doubled since October 2026. This means attackers can switch tactics quickly if one method fails, leaving organisations with less time to detect and stop the intrusion. The NCSC sees this as a significant shift in how cyber threats operate.

To protect against these faster attacks, the NCSC urges organisations to install critical security updates on internet-facing systems within 24 hours of release. It also advises reducing the number of systems exposed to the internet and ensuring that any remaining access is protected by multi-factor authentication or passkeys.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. The reports focus on organisations and critical infrastructure operators in Switzerland. If you work for a business or use a service that relies on critical infrastructure, you should check for official notices from that provider or your employer regarding security updates.

What to do

  • Ensure your online accounts use multi-factor authentication or passkeys to add an extra layer of security.
  • Keep your devices and software up to date by installing security updates as soon as they are available.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for each account to prevent attackers from guessing your details.
