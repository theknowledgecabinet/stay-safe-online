FIFA confirms cyberattack exposed personal and business data

FIFA says hackers accessed a limited set of personal and business information, and the organisation is working with law enforcement.

Friday 27 November 2026, 6.17am

What happened

FIFA has confirmed it was the victim of a cyberattack. The world governing body for soccer said hackers gained access to a "limited set of personal and business information".

FIFA did not say when the attack happened or exactly what details were taken. It is now working closely with relevant authorities, including law enforcement.

Last week, a group calling itself the Handsome Boy Hacking Club posted a screenshot on X. It claimed these were FIFA files. Three days later, the group appeared to take credit for the hack, saying more than 12 gigabytes of internal documents had been leaked.

FIFA condemned the "unlawful acquisition and publication of information" in a statement. It said such actions are a serious violation of privacy and can have significant consequences for the individuals concerned.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say who is affected. You should check your email for any official notices from FIFA or services you use. You can also review your bank statements for any unusual activity.

What to do

  • Watch for official communications from FIFA or other organisations you use.
  • Be careful with messages asking for your login details, as scammers may use this news to trick you.
  • Check your bank statements for any unrecognised transactions.
