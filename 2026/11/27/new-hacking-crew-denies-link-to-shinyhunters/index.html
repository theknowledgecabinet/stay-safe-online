New hacking crew denies link to ShinyHunters

A group called Inception says it is not the same as the ShinyHunters crew, despite looking very similar.

Friday 27 November 2026, 3.59pm

What happened

Security researchers have noticed a new criminal group called Inception. They believe this group might be a rebrand of the ShinyHunters crew, which is known for stealing data and demanding money.

However, Inception has publicly rejected this idea. The group says it has no affiliation with ShinyHunters. It claims to be a separate entity, even though it looks and acts in a very similar way.

This situation is confusing for experts trying to track cybercrime. When groups change their names or logos, it can be hard to know who is responsible for specific attacks.

For now, there is no confirmation from authorities that these two groups are the same. The debate is currently between security researchers and the criminal group itself.

Am I affected?

The sources don't say who is affected. You can check your account emails for unusual activity, review your bank statements for unrecognised charges, and look for official notices from the services you use.

What to do

  • Keep your passwords unique and use a password manager to store them safely.
  • Enable two-factor authentication on your important accounts for an extra layer of security.
  • Be careful with unexpected messages asking for money or personal details.
