Old ShinyHunters account posts on leak site

A post signed with an old key suggests the ShinyHunters group may be active again, but the signature does not prove the post is genuine.

Friday 27 November 2026, 4.00pm

What happened

A post appeared on a website called Exploit, using an account from the ShinyHunters group. The post claims to explain what happened and what will happen next. It was signed with the group's original digital key, known as a PGP key.

However, this signature does not prove the post is genuine. Reports say the key leaked months ago, so anyone could have used it. The post also references other details, but the sources do not confirm if any new data was stolen.

Because the key is no longer secure, the signature does not prove anything regarding the authenticity of the post. It is just a claim made by someone using an old account. Readers should treat this as unverified news until more is known.

Am I affected?

The sources don't say who is affected. You can check your account emails for security alerts, review your bank statements for unusual activity, and look for notices from services you use.

What to do

  • Do not click links in unexpected messages claiming to be from security groups.
  • Check your email for official alerts from services you use.
  • Keep your passwords unique and use a password manager.
