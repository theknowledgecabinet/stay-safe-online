Rackspace closes Spot cloud service after security breach

Rackspace is shutting down its Spot platform after hackers accessed customer Kubernetes clusters and secrets, giving users until 8 December to move their files.

Friday 27 November 2026, 6.21am

What happened

Rackspace has decided to close its Spot cloud platform after a security breach. The company said hackers used stolen login details to get into the system on 17 November. This allowed them to access customer Kubernetes clusters and secrets and create unauthorized pods in the system.

Rackspace found the problem on 18 November after a customer reported it. They quickly blocked access to the management area to secure the system. Service was restored on 21 November after a period of downtime.

The company says the breach was limited to the Spot platform. It has not found evidence that hackers got into Rackspace's main systems or other customers' environments. However, Rackspace is shutting the service down permanently on 8 December 2026.

Customers must download their data and move their services to a new platform before the deadline. After that date, the data will no longer be accessible. Some customers have asked Rackspace to reconsider, but the company has not said it will change its mind.

Am I affected?

The sources say this affects customers who use the Rackspace Spot cloud platform. The sources do not say who is affected.

What to do

  • Check if you use the Rackspace Spot cloud platform.
  • If you do, download your data and move your services before 8 December 2026.
  • Review your account emails for any security notices from Rackspace.
