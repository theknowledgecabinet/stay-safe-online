Signal blocks AI assistants from reading your chats

Signal has added new protections to stop built-in AI tools from capturing your private messages on iPhone and Android.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 8.06pm

What happened

Signal has announced new security features for its messaging app. These updates stop AI assistants, such as Siri on iPhones and Gemini on Android phones, from reading your conversations while they perform tasks.

Signal’s president, Meredith Whittaker, said these AI tools can capture chat content as it appears on the screen. This puts private data outside of Signal’s control and exposes everyone in the conversation. The company spent months designing these protections to block the ways AI systems access app data.

However, Signal warns that AI agents might still find other ways to access your chats. This is a particular concern on Mac computers, where Signal currently cannot block screenshots. For Mac users, the company advises adding Signal to the 'Excluded Apps' list in your system settings to stop Siri from accessing your messages.

Signal has asked Apple, Google, and Microsoft to provide clear documentation on how AI accesses data. This would allow app developers to easily opt out of AI access to screens and files. The company says these new protections are automatically activated when possible.

Am I affected?

The sources say this affects people who use the Signal app on iOS 27 or supported Android devices. You can check if the protection is active by going to Settings > App Security in the Signal app. If you use a Mac, you should check your System Settings to ensure Signal is excluded from Siri.

What to do

  • Update the Signal app to the latest available version.
  • On iPhones, go to Settings > App Security and make sure 'Block Siri AI' is enabled.
  • On Macs, add Signal to the 'Excluded Apps' list under System Settings > Siri.
