Careers360 data breach

Data allegedly taken from the Indian education and careers platform Careers360 appeared on a public hacking forum.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.46pm

What happened

In November 2026, data allegedly taken from the Indian education and careers platform Careers360 appeared on a public hacking forum. The 22M records included 3.6M unique email addresses alongside names, phone numbers, genders and caste categories, and most also contained students' entrance exam results, including ranks and marks.

The breach was added to the Have I Been Pwned database on 20 November 2026. The source attributes the incident to a threat actor known as 'Threat Actor 888'.

Am I affected?

The data includes email addresses and phone numbers.

What to do

  • Change your password for Careers360 and any other accounts where you used the same password.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) for your accounts to add an extra layer of security.
