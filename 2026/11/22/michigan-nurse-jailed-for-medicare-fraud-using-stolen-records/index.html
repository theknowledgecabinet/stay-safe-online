Michigan nurse jailed for Medicare fraud using stolen records

A nurse in the US was sentenced to prison for billing Medicare for services that never happened, using stolen patient details.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.43pm

What happened

Ruby Scott, a nurse from Farmington Hills, Michigan, has been sentenced to four years in federal prison. She owned a home health care company called Delta Home Health Care LLC. According to a press release from the Justice Department, she used stolen patient records to bill Medicare for fake services. The total value of the fraud scheme was reported as $1.6 million. Fox2 in Detroit reported that the sentencing took place on Thursday.

Am I affected?

This story concerns a specific fraud case in the US. The sources do not say if UK residents are affected or if their data was involved. It serves as a reminder that personal health records can be misused if not protected.

What to do

  • Check your bank statements and health records regularly for any services you did not receive.
  • Keep your passwords unique and use a password manager to protect your accounts.
  • Be careful about who you share your personal and medical details with.
