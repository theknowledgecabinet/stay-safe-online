Ofcom investigates Meta over Instagram feature launch

The UK regulator is checking if Meta broke safety laws by launching a new Instagram feature without assessing risks to children.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.45pm

What happened

Ofcom has opened a formal investigation into Meta. The regulator wants to know if the company failed to follow UK online safety laws.

In May, Meta launched a feature called Instants on Instagram. This allows users to share content that disappears after it has been viewed. Under the UK’s Online Safety Act, tech firms must check for risks before making big changes to their services.

Ofcom says providers must assess how their features might expose children to harmful material. They also must check for risks of illegal content. The regulator warned earlier this year that firms could face action if they skipped these checks.

George Lusty, Director of Enforcement at Ofcom, compared the process to testing a new car. He said safety must be built in from the start, not added later. If the investigation finds a breach, Ofcom can order fixes or impose fines of up to £18 million or 10% of global revenue.

Am I affected?

The sources do not say if any specific harm has occurred yet, only that the risk assessment may have been missing.

What to do

  • Check your privacy settings on Instagram to control who sees your content.
  • Talk to your children about sharing disappearing messages and the risks involved.
  • Keep an eye on Ofcom’s updates for the outcome of this investigation.
