Snapchat turns off 'Find Friends' for children by default

Ofcom says Snapchat is changing its settings to better protect UK children from online strangers.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 8.05pm

What happened

Ofcom has confirmed that Snapchat is now turning off its 'Find Friends' feature for child users by default. This means children will be better protected from stranger danger online.

Oliver Griffiths, Ofcom’s Online Safety Group Director, described this as an important and welcome improvement. He noted that the change is long overdue and that Ofcom will hold the company to account to ensure the rollout is completed this month.

Snapchat has also told Ofcom that highly effective age checks will be applied by the end of this year. This will ensure that all under-18s benefit from these new safety measures. Ofcom says it will monitor this progress closely.

The regulator sent a clear message to tech companies: tackling the risks of grooming to children is a non-negotiable. Companies that fail to step up to the mark will face enforcement action.

Am I affected?

This affects children who use Snapchat in the UK. You can check if your child's 'Find Friends' feature is turned off by looking at their app settings. The sources don't say who is affected beyond child users of the platform.

What to do

  • Check your child's Snapchat settings to ensure 'Find Friends' is turned off.
  • Review your child's privacy settings to limit who can contact them.
  • Talk to your child about online safety and the importance of not sharing personal details with strangers.
