Bell Canada confirms data breach affecting business customers

A Canadian telecoms firm says a hacker stole business contact details, but says no personal passwords or sensitive files were taken.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.44pm

What happened

Bell Canada has confirmed that its systems were breached on 17 November 2026. The company says an "agentic" threat actor accessed its networks. This term suggests the attacker used AI-powered tools to carry out the attack.

According to Bell, the hacker stole data from a system used to order and set up telephone connections. The information may have been stolen includes business contact details, such as names, job titles, and work email addresses. It also includes some technical details about network connections.

Bell told BleepingComputer that the data did not contain passwords, highly sensitive personal information, or file attachments. The company said it quickly secured its systems and that customer services have not been affected.

Bell has not said how many businesses were affected or who is responsible for the attack. It is sharing security details with the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security to help other organisations check for similar threats.

Am I affected?

This breach affects business customers of Bell Canada in Canada. If you work for a company that uses Bell for its phone or internet connections, your work contact details may have been stolen. The sources do not say if any UK organisations were affected.

What to do

  • Be careful of unexpected emails or calls claiming to be from your telecoms provider.
  • Do not share your password or personal details if someone contacts you out of the blue.
  • Check with your IT team if your business uses Bell Canada services to see if you need to take further action.
