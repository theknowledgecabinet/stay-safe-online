Cleverbridge breach exposes payment card details

Cleverbridge says hackers stole payment card details, including security codes, from its online sales platform.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.42pm

What happened

Cleverbridge is an online service that helps software companies sell products and process payments. The company says it detected unauthorised activity on 4 November 2026. It temporarily took its systems offline to contain the breach.

Cleverbridge confirmed that a third party copied payment card data. A notification sent to customers reveals that the stolen information includes card numbers, expiry dates, and CVV codes.

The company says it did not steal other personal data, such as names or billing addresses. It has not said how many customers were affected or how the attackers got in. Cleverbridge has told data protection authorities and law enforcement about the incident.

The company is working with TransUnion to send out notifications. It is also offering free credit monitoring to affected customers to help them track their accounts.

Am I affected?

You are affected if you bought software or services through Cleverbridge. The company has not listed which specific businesses were impacted. Check your email for a notification from Cleverbridge or TransUnion.

What to do

  • Check your bank statements for any transactions you do not recognise.
  • Contact your bank or card issuer to report the breach.
  • Ask your bank if they recommend replacing your card.
  • Keep an eye on your credit report for signs of fraud.
