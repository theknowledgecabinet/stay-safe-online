Singapore YMCA portal hit by data breach

The YMCA of Singapore says its online application system was breached, exposing some personal details.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.44pm

What happened

The YMCA of Singapore has confirmed a cybersecurity breach. It affected the online portal for its Youth For Causes programme. The organisation announced this on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The breach involved the database linked to the application portal. This database held personal information about users. Some of this data included full or partial NRIC numbers.

The YMCA sent an email to participants and mentors on Saturday.

Am I affected?

This breach affects people who used the Youth For Causes portal in Singapore.

What to do

  • Check your email for official messages from the YMCA of Singapore if you applied.
  • Be careful of phishing emails that ask for your ID number after a breach.
  • Keep your phone and computer software up to date to stay safe online.
