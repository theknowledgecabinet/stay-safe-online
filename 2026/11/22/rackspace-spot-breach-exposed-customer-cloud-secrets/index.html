Rackspace Spot breach exposed customer cloud secrets

Hackers used stolen login details to access a cloud platform, potentially exposing sensitive passwords and keys for business customers.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.43pm

What happened

Rackspace has warned that hackers breached its Rackspace Spot cloud platform. The company said attackers used leaked credentials to access parts of the system on 17 November 2026. This allowed them to act like legitimate users in some customer organisations.

The attackers could create new workloads and read 'Kubernetes Secrets'. These are secure files that store sensitive information such as passwords, authentication tokens, and API keys. Rackspace warned that the hackers could have read any secret stored in an affected user's clusters.

Rackspace says it learned of the intrusion on 18 November after a customer report. The company has since revoked affected tokens, removed unauthorised workloads, and rotated keys. It also strengthened access controls and monitoring to prevent further issues.

The breach caused an extended outage of the Rackspace Spot administration console. This temporarily prevented customers from managing their clusters. Service was fully restored on 21 November 2026.

Am I affected?

This affects organisations that use the Rackspace Spot cloud platform to manage their applications. If you are a customer, you should have received a notification from Rackspace. The sources do not say how many customers were affected or if personal data was stolen.

What to do

  • Check if you received a notification from Rackspace about this breach.
  • If you are an affected customer, reissue your Spot API tokens and replace refresh tokens.
  • Rotate any credentials stored in your cluster secrets to ensure they are safe.
  • Review your logs for any unusual activity dating back to 17 November 2026.
