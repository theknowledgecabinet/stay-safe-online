Phone company fined £437,500 for letting scammers use its numbers

Ofcom has fined Tismi for failing to stop scammers from using phone numbers it provided, putting consumers at risk of fraud.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.41pm

What happened

Ofcom has fined telecoms provider Tismi £437,500. The regulator found that Tismi failed to take proper steps to prevent its phone numbers from being misused by scammers. This failure lasted from November 2022 to January 2025. During this time, approximately 366,000 numbers were exposed to potential misuse.

Phone companies must check their business customers to stop scammers getting valid numbers. They must also monitor for misuse and handle reports properly. Ofcom found that Tismi did not carry out these checks or have the right systems in place. It also lacked a formal process for dealing with reports of misuse.

Ofcom Director of Enforcement George Lusty said there is no excuse for failing to put proper checks in place. He stated that companies putting customers at the mercy of scammers will face consequences. The fine will be passed to HM Treasury. Tismi received a 30% discount on the fine because it admitted liability.

As part of the decision, Tismi must now prove it is following the rules. It must provide details of its new checks and monitoring processes. Ofcom has also opened a new investigation into another company, Xtra Phones UK, over similar concerns about number misuse.

Am I affected?

The sources do not list specific numbers used by scammers, so you cannot check if a specific number was involved.

What to do

  • Be wary of unexpected calls or texts asking for personal or financial details.
  • Report scam calls and texts to Ofcom using their online Consumer Hub.
  • Check Ofcom's website for tips on how to protect yourself against scams.
