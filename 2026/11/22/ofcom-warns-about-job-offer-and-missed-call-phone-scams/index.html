Ofcom warns about 'job offer' and missed call phone scams

Criminals are using automated voices and quick hang-ups to trick you into calling back, so you should hang up and report these calls.

Sunday 22 November 2026, 6.40pm

What happened

Ofcom has warned the public about two common types of scam phone calls. One uses an automated voice to claim you have a job offer. The other is a call from an unknown number that hangs up after just a few moments.

These calls are designed to make you call the number back. Once you are on the line, criminals try to pressure you into giving away sensitive personal details or making payments to accounts they control. You could also accidentally call a premium-rate number and run up a large bill.

Ofcom says the number of people reporting suspicious calls has fallen over the last four years. However, the regulator is still investigating four telecoms providers as part of its ongoing work to tackle phone scams.

An Ofcom spokesperson said: “Tackling this complex problem requires a joined-up approach from Government, other regulators, law enforcement and industry.” They noted that telecoms companies are already blocking millions of scam calls every month.

Am I affected?

You will know if you get an automated voice offering a job or a brief call from an unknown number that ends quickly.

What to do

  • Hang up immediately if you receive one of these calls.
  • Do not give out any personal details or make payments.
  • Report the call to 7726.
